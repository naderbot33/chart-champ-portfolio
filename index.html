Historical plans are not current buy alerts. Entry, target, and stop outcomes require dated evidence.

No setups match these filters. An old or unreviewed idea is never shown as an active signal.
How to read ChartChamp

Scanner hits are candidates. Setups are conditional plans. The Community Portfolio records confirmed decisions separately. A later price touch does not prove that an entry condition or exit happened in the required order.

Research keeps the date and context of each source. Chart updates do not refresh company fundamentals. Historical and incomplete reviews are labeled; unverified outcomes are excluded from performance claims.

Setup follow-ups should record Watching, Triggered, Take profit level 1, Take profit level 2, Stopped, or Expired with dated evidence. A review-overdue label asks for follow-up; it does not invent a trade result.
